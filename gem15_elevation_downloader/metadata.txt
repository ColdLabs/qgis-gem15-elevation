about=Downloads GEM-15, the experimental near-global elevation model from Google Research, for the current map extent or the polygons of a layer: DTM (bare earth), DSM (surface, including buildings and trees) and nDSM (DSM minus DTM, the height of what stands on the ground), at 15 m.

    Coverage: 60 S to 84 N, except Russia, China, Mongolia, Greenland and Antarctica. India is offset on purpose by Google. The DTM is less accurate under dense forest.

    Google publishes one Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF per 1-degree cell. The plugin reads only the part each file needs for the area, so a town takes seconds and nothing is cached. Output is a GeoTIFF per product, in the UTM zone of the area unless you choose a CRS. Loaded layers are coloured by elevation (DTM, DSM) or by height above ground (nDSM, see-through under 2 m). No extra Python packages are needed.

    Data licence: GEM-15 is published by Google under CC-BY 4.0 and requires the attribution "This dataset is produced by Google". The plugin writes it into every output file. This plugin is not affiliated with Google.

    Runs as a Processing algorithm, so it also works in models, batch mode and qgis_process.
